Beyond Roles · #74

If I enter a room, I have to be the most unique person in it.

Tavish means it. A student, a founder, a cosplayer, and a litter-picking organiser. All at once.

  • Nationalsfirst handmade cosplay
  • SpoGomilitter-picking sport
  • Japanwhere he wants to build
二

Who is he?

Tavish SK is a student at BMS who recently started his own venture, Zensation Dojo. “I haven’t done a lot of things,” he says, and then lists all of this:

  • FounderZensation Dojo
  • Studentat BMS
  • DevOpsintern
  • 2 NGOscore organiser
  • Footballcaptain & coach
  • SpoGomicore committee
三

How did it start?

Bishop Cotton Boys’ School, then Kumarans for PUC — “the best decision I could ever take.” A curious kid who kept asking why.

“Education from an institute and being literate are two different things.”

Then came Comic Con.

四

How did cosplay happen?

Four years ago he went to his first Comic Con wearing the flashiest things he could find at home — and people assumed he was cosplaying. “Okay, let me make a story around it.” He created his own character, Jigoku no Punk.

  1. Outfitwhatever was flashiest
  2. Own characterJigoku no Punk
  3. Handmadestitched from scratch
  4. Nationalsselected

“Anime changed my life.”

五

And he picks up litter as a sport.

SpoGomi is a Japanese competition where teams race to collect rubbish. He was a supervisor in 2025 and is now on its core committee. The hardest part? People asking why he is cleaning a park that isn’t his.

In Japan, which he visited in 2024, he saw busy streets left spotless and children who clean their own classrooms. That is the civic sense he wants here.

六

What else does he love?

He loves science and runs a movement called Cosmic Unravelers. Music, poetry, chess and football fill the rest.

  • Music
  • Poetry
  • Chess
  • Football

His best memory? Going to Japan, in 2024. Next he wants to learn as much as he can across every field, and one day build a startup in Japan.
